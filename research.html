01

COMBUSTION EMISSIONS

How open burning deteorates urban and regional air quality?

Unregulated open burning emissions are chemically highly complex. Constituted of thousands of organic compounds, these undergo atmospheric chemical transformations that can impact climate as well as human health over urban to larger spatial scales. We investigate complex organic emissions and characterize their environmental fate at the molecular level to constrain their influence on our quality of life.

02

NON-TRADITIONAL EMISSIONS

Volatile chemical products — What doesn't burn yet causes air pollution?

Following decades of regulatory policies, air pollution sources are evolving around the world. We study the emerging non-combustion sources that often surround us in our daily lives and are increasingly dominating air quality impacts in megacities worldwide.

03

MEASUREMENT SCIENCE / DEVELOPMENT OF NEW ANALYTICAL TECHNIQUES

How well can we understand aerosol formation at the molecular level?

We develop and implement analytical approaches for molecular characterization of organic aerosol and their reactive gas-phase precursors via state-of-the-art mass spectrometry. This allows us to develop new insights into variuos chemical mechanisms that lead to environmental issues from human activities.

04

DATA MODELING & APPLICATION OF ARTIFICIAL NEURAL NETWORKS IN UNDERSTANDING THE ENVIRONMENTAL SYSTEMS

Can chemical data provide actionable conclusions for climate and human health?

We employ source apportionment, machine learning and atmospheric modeling tools on our field and lab measurements to reveal the processes that matter for air quality, human health and climate.
