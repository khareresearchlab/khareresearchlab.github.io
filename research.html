COMBUSTION EMISSIONS

How open burning deteorates urban and regional air quality?

NON-TRADITIONAL SOURCES OF AIR POLLUTION

Volatile consumer products - What doesn't burn and yet cause air pollution?

Following decades of regulatory policies, air pollution sources are evolving around the world. We study the emerging non-combustion sources that are increasingly dominating air quality impacts in megacities worldwide.

MEASUREMENT SCIENCE / DEVELOPMENT OF ANALYTICAL TECHNIQUES

How well can we understand aerosol formation at the molecular level?

We develop and implement analytical approaches for molecular characterization of organic aerosol and their reactive gas-phase precursors via state-of-the-art mass spectrometer.
