Data source & calculation

Source: IHME, Global Burden of Disease 2023, as reported in State of Global Air 2025 (HEI / IHME). Reference-year estimates: approximately 2 million deaths in India and 7.9 million worldwide in 2023. The report describes India’s total as more than 2 million; this display uses a rounded 2 million baseline. Includes the combined burden from ambient particulate matter, household air pollution and ozone.

Daily average = rounded annual estimate ÷ 365. The year-to-date projection multiplies this average by completed calendar days since 1 January in India Standard Time. Values are rounded to the nearest ten. This assumes a constant daily burden and does not represent seasonal variation, current-year observations or uncertainty intervals. Only the projection updates daily; source estimates require revision when a new dataset is adopted.

OUR APPROACH

From emissions to impacts
Why air chemistry matters?

The atmosphere is a complex reactor in which hundreds of thousands of precursors transform via different oxidation processes. Gases form airborne particles - particles can form clouds, deposit in our lungs or impact the broader ecosystem. We combine field measurements, laboratory experiments and advanced data wrangling and analysis methods to trace reactive organic emissions from their sources through chemical transformation to effects on air quality.
