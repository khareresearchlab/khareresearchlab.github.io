PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR

Dr. Peeyush Khare

Assistant Professor · Kotak School of Sustainability, IIT Kanpur

Peeyush Khare is an atmospheric chemist studying organic aerosols, reactive emissions and the processes that connect sources to air quality and climate. His work combines field observations, laboratory analysis and quantitative methods.
