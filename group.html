PRINCIPAL INVESTIGATOR

Dr. Peeyush Khare

Assistant Professor · Kotak School of Sustainability, IIT Kanpur

Ph.D. Chemical and Environmental Engineering, Yale University, USA.

Peeyush Khare is an environmental engineer and an atmospheric chemist studying organic aerosols, reactive emissions and the processes that connect sources to air quality, human health and climate. His work combines field observations, laboratory experiments and advanced quantitative methods.
